---
title: "Elixir Open Source Stewards"
---

The Elixir Open Source Stewards is a program that recognizes and encourages companies making sustained investments in Elixir’s open-source ecosystem.

Companies can participate by assigning members of their own team or sponsoring external developers to work substantially on open-source Elixir projects. To apply or recommend a company, contact [stewards@elixir-lang.org](mailto:stewards@elixir-lang.org).

## Eligibility

A participating company must meet one of the following commitments:

- Support at least one developer working full-time on open-source Elixir projects; or
- Support at least two developers who each spend at least 60% of their time on open-source Elixir projects.

Eligible work includes libraries, frameworks, developer tools, and other projects that benefit the broader Elixir ecosystem.

Open-source applications are not included in the program. While we support companies that build Elixir applications in the open, as they help the community learn from real-world usage and strengthen the ecosystem, this program specifically recognizes investment in reusable libraries, frameworks, and tooling.

The Elixir team will reach out to each participating company every six months to ensure it continues to meet the program’s criteria.
